snag – Privacy Policy

App: snag (Android, package app.snag.play) · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Your library stays on your device

Videos, tags, notes, collections and transcripts are stored only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud. Image recognition and speech recognition run on the device. Nothing about you or your library is sent to the maker of snag.

When snag uses the internet

  • To load a video from the platform whose link you pasted or shared. The link goes to that platform, as it would in a browser.
  • For accounts and playlists you follow in the app: to check that platform for new posts, as often as you set.
  • Once, to download the speech model (about 60 MB) if you turn speech recognition on.
  • If you buy snag Pro: the purchase is made through Google Play. The app asks the Play Store on your phone whether your Google account owns the purchase. Payment data stays with Google; the app never sees it.

Data collected by a component from Google

snag recognises what is in a video with Google's ML Kit, on the device. Your videos and images are not uploaded for this. ML Kit itself sends diagnostic and usage data to Google: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), the app's package name and version, an identifier for the installation that is not meant to identify you or your device, performance figures such as how long a recognition took, which function ran, and error codes. Google uses this to maintain and improve ML Kit. The transfer is encrypted. It is not passed on to others and the maker of snag has no access to it. See Google's ML Kit terms and Google's privacy policy.

What snag does not do

No ads. No advertising ID. No account. No sale of data. No analytics of its own.

Reports are sent by you

A problem report or crash report leaves the phone only when you tap Send, through your own mail app or the share menu. You see the full text first: the link, an error code and version numbers (app, download engine, Android, phone model).

Backup

A backup is one ZIP file that you place yourself, for example in your cloud storage. It contains your library data and, if you choose so, the video files, including those in the private vault. Keep it somewhere you trust.

Deleting your data

Everything snag stores is on your phone. Delete single videos in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. There is nothing to delete on a server, because there is none.

Your responsibility

Save only what you are allowed to save, for your own use. The rights to a video stay with the people who made it.
